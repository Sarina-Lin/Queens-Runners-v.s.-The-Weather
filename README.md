# Queens-Runners-v.s.-The-Weather
Have you ever felt that the weather has affected your running experience? During a cross-country meet, runners may encounter extreme heat or cold, rain or sunshine, and different levels of humidity. However, how are these conditions associated with race performance?

This project investigates the relationship between weather conditions and 5k varsity runners' cross-country finishing times among Queens Public Schools Athletic League (PSAL) varsity runners across three seasons (2023-2026). Using historical race results and weather data, I analyzed whether temperature, humidity, wind speed, and precipitation were associated with runners' finishing times.
